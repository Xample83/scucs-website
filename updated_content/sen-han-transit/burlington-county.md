# SEN-HAN Transit — Burlington County

> **Meta description:** Book SEN-HAN Transit in Burlington County for medical rides, shopping, nutrition sites, and veterans VA transport. Seniors 60+ and eligible disabled riders. Call (877) 603-5111 to reserve.

Since 2012, SEN-HAN Transit has provided Burlington County residents with dependable rides to medical appointments, shopping, nutrition sites, and more.

## Who can ride?

- Burlington County residents **age 60+**, or
- Individuals certified as disabled by SCUCS (physician form required)

[Disabled Application](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/disabledappformBUR0316.pdf)

## How to reserve a ride

Call **[(877) 603-5111](tel:8776035111)** Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM.

### Important numbers

| Need | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Ride reservations | 877-603-5111 |
| Shopping reservations | 856-255-5348 |
| Email | senhanbc@scucs.org |
| Fax | 856-255-5396 |

### Medical rides

- Available Monday–Friday based on availability
- Reserve **two days to two weeks** ahead

### Personal business rides

- Available Monday–Friday based on availability
- Reserve **three days** ahead
- Destinations must be within **five miles** of your home

## How to reserve a shopping ride

Weekly shopping serves **38 Burlington County municipalities** for residents age 60+.

1. Call **[(856) 255-5348](tel:8562555348)** between 6 AM and 5 PM.
2. Call **the working day before** your trip.
3. Leave your name, address, town, and preferred day.

## How to reserve veterans transportation

VA Hospital shuttles run Tuesday–Friday.

- Arrival at the Philadelphia VA: about **9:30 AM**
- Return departure: **2:30 PM**

### Pickup locations and times

| Pickup location | Time |
| --- | --- |
| American Legion Post 294, Pemberton | 7:15 AM |
| Crossroads Plaza, Lumberton | 7:15 AM |
| KMart Shopping Center, Moorestown (Rt. 38) | 8:15 AM |
| Shoppes at Cinnaminson (Rt. 130) | 8:30 AM |

Call **[(877) 603-5111](tel:8776035111)** by **11:00 AM the day before**. Cancel at least 24 hours ahead.
