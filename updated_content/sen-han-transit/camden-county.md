# SEN-HAN Transit — Camden County

> **Meta description:** Book SEN-HAN Transit rides in Camden County for medical appointments, shopping, and personal trips. Seniors 60+ and eligible disabled riders welcome. Call (856) 456-3344 to reserve.

SEN-HAN Transit is Camden County’s provider of transportation for seniors and people with disabilities. We help you get to medical appointments, shopping, and everyday destinations—safely and reliably.

## Who can ride?

- Camden County residents **age 60+**, or
- Individuals certified as disabled by SCUCS (physician form required)

[Disabled Application (English)](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/disabledappformCAM0316.pdf)

## How to reserve a ride

1. Call **[(856) 456-3344](tel:8564563344)** Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM.
2. Reserve **two days to two weeks** ahead.
3. Confirm your ride before your trip date.

**All rides must be confirmed.**

## Medical rides

Non-emergency medical transportation is available Monday–Friday, based on availability. Reserve two days to two weeks in advance.

### Local shuttle (Voorhees)

Weekday shuttle stops include:

- Pavilions of Voorhees
- Tatem Brown
- Carnie and Centennial Boulevard offices
- Virtua Hospital
- Genesis Nursing Home
- Select locations on Evesham and Cooper Roads

The shuttle makes three stops per weekday.

### Philadelphia hospital shuttle

In partnership with Gloucester County, service runs Monday–Thursday to:

- Cooper Hospital
- Hahnemann Hospital
- Wills Eye Hospital
- Jefferson Hospital
- Graduate Hospital
- Children’s Hospital
- HUP
- Scheie Eye Institute
- VA Hospital

Fridays go to the VA Hospital only.

## Personal business rides

- Available Monday–Friday based on availability
- Reserve **three days** in advance

## How to reserve a shopping ride

Weekly shopping service covers **24 Camden County municipalities** and is open to all county residents.

1. Call **[(856) 456-1148](tel:8564561148)** between 6 AM and 6 PM.
2. Call **the working day before** your trip.
3. Leave your name, address, town, and preferred shopping day.

## Camden City special shopping

Service for Camden City seniors and disabled residents runs 1–2 days per week in:

- Cramer Hill
- East Camden
- Fairview
- North Camden
- Parkside
- South Camden
- Whitman Park

Preregistered riders call **[(856) 456-1148](tel:8564561148)** the working day before. Suggested donation: **$2 one way**.

## How to apply for veterans transportation

Veterans and spouses may request non-emergency medical rides (first come, first served).

Eligibility is set by the Camden County Department of Military and Veterans Affairs at 3 Collier Drive, Blackwood.

**Call [(856) 374-5801](tel:8563745801)** to apply.

**Please note:** Tipping is not permitted.
