SCUCS offers counseling for South Jersey homeowners age **62 and older** so you can make an informed choice with confidence.

## What is a reverse mortgage?

A Home Equity Conversion Mortgage (HECM)—often called a reverse mortgage—lets eligible homeowners age 62+ convert home equity into:

- A line of credit, or
- Monthly income

### Basic requirements

- You are age **62 or older**
- The home is your primary residence
- The home is a single-family house or HUD-approved condominium

The loan is typically repaid when you permanently leave the home. Counseling is required before you apply, and we encourage family members to attend.
