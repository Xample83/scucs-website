# Emergency Services

> **Meta description:** SCUCS Emergency Services help Camden and Gloucester County residents with food, shopping support, heater repairs, medical equipment loans, utility bills, and rental assistance. Call to apply.

When a crisis hits—empty cupboards, a broken heater, a shut-off notice—help is available.

SCUCS Emergency Services support eligible Camden and Gloucester County residents with food, housing stability, and essential household needs.

## Emergency food bags

Camden County seniors **age 60+** can pick up up to two bags of non-perishable food each month. Home delivery may be available if a volunteer serves your area.

**Camden City residents:** Please call the Camden City Office on Aging at **[(856) 757-7339](tel:8567577339)** for local food banks. This SCUCS food program does not serve Camden City.

Want to donate? See our [requested food donations list](http://scucs.org/requested-food-donations/).

### How to get emergency food

Call and ask for the Volunteer Coordinator.

**Call [(856) 456-1121, Ext. 160](tel:8564561121)**

## Shut-in shopping

Homebound Camden County seniors can receive bi-weekly shopping help from a screened volunteer (up to 20 grocery or pharmacy items).

- You pay for your purchases
- Service depends on volunteer availability

### How to request shut-in shopping

Call and ask for the Volunteer Coordinator.

**Call [(856) 456-1121, Ext. 160](tel:8564561121)**

## How to apply for heater assistance

Camden County residents **age 60+** may qualify for heating and air conditioning (HVAC) repairs or replacements when funds are available.

We will ask about:

- Household income and expenses
- Who lives in the home

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)** to check fund availability and start an application.

## Lending Closet for medical equipment

Borrow equipment temporarily if you cannot afford to buy it, including:

- Wheelchairs
- Walkers and canes
- Shower seats
- Rollators and knee walkers
- Commodes

A limited supply of adult diapers may also be available. Donations are welcome and tax-deductible.

### How to borrow or donate equipment

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**

## How to apply for utility or fuel help

Camden and Gloucester County residents may receive help with:

- Heating oil (up to 100 gallons)
- Past-due utility bills when facing a disconnect notice

Help is subject to available funds.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)** to ask about current funding and apply.

## How to apply for rental assistance

Residents of Camden and Gloucester Counties may receive help with:

- A security deposit
- First month’s rent
- Back rent

Help is available when funds allow.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)** to learn more and apply.
