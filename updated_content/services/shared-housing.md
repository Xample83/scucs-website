# Shared Housing

> **Meta description:** SCUCS Shared Housing helps Camden County seniors 60+ and people with disabilities find affordable housing options. Call (856) 456-1121 to learn how we can help.

Safe, affordable housing can be hard to find. We are here to help you explore options that fit your needs and budget.

SCUCS Shared Housing supports Camden County residents age **60 and older**—and people who are permanently physically disabled—in finding housing that costs less than typical market-rate rent.

## Who this program serves

- Camden County residents age **60+**
- Camden County residents who are **permanently physically disabled**
- Anyone who may benefit from a shared housing arrangement

## How we help

Through partnerships with local apartment communities and subsidized housing providers, we help you:

- Identify housing that fits your needs
- Explore more affordable options
- Take the next step with confidence

[Download the Shared Housing brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/comprehensiveHousing-Counseling-brochure.pdf)

## How to get housing help

Call and ask about **Shared Housing**. We will listen to your situation and help you explore options.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**
