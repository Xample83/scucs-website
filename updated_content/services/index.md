# Our Services

> **Meta description:** Explore SCUCS programs for seniors and people with disabilities in South Jersey—travel, transit, housing, emergency help, and more. Find the right support and call us to get started.

At Senior Citizens United Community Services (SCUCS), we help older adults and people with disabilities live safely, independently, and with dignity.

Whether you need a ride, a helping hand at home, or guidance through a difficult moment—we are here for you.

## How we can help

- **Travel & Tours** — Affordable day trips, overnight getaways, and cruises
- **SEN-HAN Transit** — Reliable transportation for seniors and people with disabilities
- **Case Management** — Personalized support in select Burlington County towns
- **In-Home Services** — Respite care and short-term personal care
- **Shared Housing** — Help finding affordable housing options
- **Emergency Services** — Food, utilities, rent, and medical equipment support
- **Reverse Mortgage Counseling** — Free guidance for homeowners 62 and older
- **SHIP Medicare Counseling** — Clear answers about Medicare coverage and costs
- **DDD Support Coordination** — Case management for adults with developmental disabilities
