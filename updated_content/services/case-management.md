# Case Management — Burlington County River Towns

> **Meta description:** Free SCUCS case management for seniors in Beverly, Delanco, Riverside, Riverton, and Palmyra. Get help with benefits, housing, budgeting, and community resources. Call (856) 456-1121.

Looking for someone who can help you navigate benefits, housing, and local resources? Our Case Management Program is here for seniors and caregivers in select Burlington County towns.

This program is funded by the Community Foundation of South Jersey.

## Towns we serve

- Beverly
- Delanco
- Riverside
- Riverton
- Palmyra

## How a care manager can help

- Benefits counseling
- Budget counseling
- Housing assistance
- Caregiver support
- Connections to local resources and services
- Other help based on your individual needs

## How to request case management

Call Monday–Friday, 9 AM–4 PM, and ask about the **Burlington County River Towns Case Management Program**.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**
