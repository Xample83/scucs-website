# SHIP Medicare Counseling

> **Meta description:** Free SHIP Medicare counseling for Burlington County residents. Certified counselors help with enrollment, coverage questions, costs, and billing issues. Call SCUCS at (856) 456-1121.

Confused by Medicare? You are not alone—and help is free.

Through the State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP), SCUCS offers one-on-one counseling for Medicare-eligible residents of **Burlington County**.

## How a SHIP counselor can help

- Enroll in Medicare
- Understand costs and coverage
- Compare plan options
- Resolve billing issues

Our counselors are trained, certified Medicare experts.

## How to schedule a SHIP appointment

Call and ask for **SHIP counseling**. We will match you with a trained counselor.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**
