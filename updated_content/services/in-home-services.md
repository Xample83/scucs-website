# In-Home Services

> **Meta description:** SCUCS In-Home Services offer respite care for caregivers and short-term personal care for seniors recovering at home in Camden and Gloucester Counties. Call to learn more.

Caring for a loved one—or recovering after an illness—can feel overwhelming. You do not have to do it alone.

SCUCS In-Home Services give older adults and people with disabilities practical support so they can stay safely at home.

## Respite Care

Need a break—even for a few hours? Respite Care gives caregivers temporary relief through options such as:

- Adult day programs
- In-home health aides
- Short-term out-of-home placements

We serve older adults and people with disabilities in **Camden and Gloucester Counties**—and we support the family members who care for them.

### Respite Care may be right for you if you

- Are the primary caregiver for an adult (18+) who is older or has a disability
- Are balancing work and caregiving
- Feel exhausted or short on time for your own needs

## Personal Care

Our Personal Care program offers short-term help for seniors recovering from an illness or injury who do not need full-time medical care, but could use assistance with daily tasks such as:

- Bathing
- Laundry
- Light housekeeping

[Download the Personal Care brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/T3brochure915.pdf)

## How to request in-home support

Call and ask about **Respite Care** or **Personal Care**. We will explain your options and next steps.
