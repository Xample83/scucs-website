# Housing Counseling

> **Meta description:** SCUCS Housing Counseling helps adults 60+ in Camden and Gloucester counties find available, affordable housing. Call (856) 456-1121 to schedule.

SCUCS Housing Counseling helps adults age **60 and older** in **Camden and Gloucester counties** find available, affordable housing. Counselors work with you one-on-one to explore options and take the next step with confidence.

## Who this program serves

- Adults age **60+** living in Camden County
- Adults age **60+** living in Gloucester County

## How we help

- Guidance on tenant rights
- Help identifying available housing units
- Support navigating housing waitlists

[Download the Housing Counseling brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Housing_Counseling.pdf)

## How to schedule housing counseling

Call and ask for **Housing Counseling**.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**
