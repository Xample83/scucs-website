# Travel & Tours FAQs

> **Meta description:** Answers to common SCUCS Travel & Tours questions—who can go, how to book, payment deadlines, cancellations, and pickup details. Call (856) 456-1121 to reserve.

Have a question about our trips? Start here.

## Who can travel with SCUCS?

Everyone is welcome. There is no age or residency requirement, and many trips work well for families.

## How are trips planned?

Trips are planned with seniors in mind, but they appeal to travelers of all ages. We focus on comfort, enjoyment, and a welcoming group experience.

## Trip books and catalogs

- Trip books are published **three times a year**
- If you traveled with us in the past year, you receive one automatically
- Pick one up free at our office or community sites
- Or mail a $3 check to SCUCS for postage
- You can also browse trips [online](http://www.communitytourstravel.com/)

## How do I book a trip?

- Visit or call our office
- Or submit a reservation request through our website

Trips cannot be booked by email or voicemail.

## When is payment due?

- **Deposit:** within one week of booking
- **Balance:** one month before the trip
- **Multiday and New York/Broadway trips:** balance due two months in advance

## How can I pay?

- Check
- Credit card

We do not accept cash.

## Where do trips leave from?

**537 Nicholson Road, Audubon, NJ 08106**, unless otherwise noted.

## How do I cancel?

Speak directly with a SCUCS representative. Cancellations cannot be made by mail, email, or voicemail.

## Should I buy trip insurance?

Yes—we strongly recommend it.
