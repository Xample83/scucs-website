# Trip Pickup Location

> **Meta description:** Find directions to the SCUCS trip pickup location at 537 Nicholson Road, Audubon, NJ. Easy access from Route 295, Berlin, Cherry Hill, and Deptford.

All SCUCS trips leave from our office unless otherwise noted.

**537 Nicholson Road**  
Audubon, NJ 08106

Parking is available in the SCUCS lot, across from Audubon Towers Senior Housing.

## Directions by area

### From Route 295

- Take Route 168 (Black Horse Pike) west to Nicholson Road
- Turn right
- The SCUCS parking lot is on the left, opposite Audubon Towers

### From Berlin

- Take Route 30 (White Horse Pike) west to Nicholson Road
- Turn left
- The lot is on the right, just after the PSE&G complex

### From Cherry Hill

- Take Route 130 south
- After Collings Road, exit left onto Route 168 (Black Horse Pike)
- At Nicholson Road (third light), exit left
- The lot is on the left, across from Audubon Towers

### From Deptford

- Take Evesham Road north
- Turn left onto Route 168 (Black Horse Pike)
- Turn right onto Nicholson Road
- The lot is on the left, opposite Audubon Towers

## Plan your arrival

Arrive a few minutes early so we can board smoothly and depart on time.
