# Travel & Tours

> **Meta description:** Join SCUCS Travel & Tours for affordable day trips, overnight getaways, and cruises. Packages include transportation, entertainment, and a trained escort. Browse the catalog and book today.

Ready for your next adventure? SCUCS Travel & Tours makes it easy—and affordable—to get out and explore.

We offer year-round day trips, overnight stays, and cruises for anyone who wants to travel with us. Most packages include transportation, entertainment, and a trained escort. Most day trips also include a meal.

Your trip also supports other SCUCS programs, including SEN-HAN Transit, in-home services, nutrition programs, and emergency help.

## What is included

- Transportation to and from your destination
- Entertainment and guided experiences
- A trained escort on every trip
- A meal on most day trips

## How to book a trip

1. Browse the [trip catalog](http://www.communitytourstravel.com/) or stop by our office.
2. Call **[(856) 456-1121, Ext. 1124](tel:8564561121)** to reserve your spot.
3. Pay your deposit within one week of booking.

**Please note:** Trips cannot be booked by email or voicemail. Speak with a staff member to reserve.
