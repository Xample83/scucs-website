# Counseling Programs

> **Meta description:** Free SCUCS counseling for benefits, housing, shared living, and Medicare (SHIP). Confidential one-on-one help for Camden County seniors and Medicare beneficiaries. Schedule an appointment today.

Whether you need help with benefits, housing, or Medicare, SCUCS counselors offer clear, confidential guidance—by phone, Zoom, or in person.

## Benefits Counseling

For Camden County residents **age 60+**, our counselors help with:

- Budgeting and debt concerns
- Applications for programs like SNAP, PAAD, and LiHEAP/USF
- Checking eligibility for additional aid

[Benefits Counseling brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Benefits_Counseling.pdf)

### How to schedule benefits counseling

Call and ask for **Benefits Counseling**.

## Housing Counseling

HUD-certified counselors help tenants and future renters in Camden and nearby counties find and keep safe, affordable housing—free of charge.

We can help with:

- Tenant rights and household budgeting
- Scam and discrimination awareness
- Finding available units and navigating waitlists

## Housing Navigation

We work to prevent homelessness by:

- Building relationships with landlords, realtors, and housing providers
- Maintaining an up-to-date list of available housing options
- Hosting landlord/tenant sessions on vouchers, leases, and available supports

### How to request housing navigation help

Call and ask about **Housing Navigation**.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**

## Shared Housing

We match Camden County homeowners with people seeking affordable housing. At least one person in each match must be **60 or older**.

What to expect:

- Personal interviews, background checks, and references
- A required Trial Weekend
- Six months of follow-up support after a match

[Shared Housing brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Shared_Housing.pdf)

### How to apply for Shared Housing

Call to learn if Shared Housing is right for you—whether you have a spare room or need an affordable place to live.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**
