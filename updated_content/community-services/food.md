# Food Programs

> **Meta description:** SCUCS Food Programs help Camden County seniors age 60+ with monthly pantry bags and bi-weekly shut-in shopping. Call (856) 456-1121 to request food assistance or volunteer shopping support.

Everyone deserves reliable access to food. SCUCS Food Programs help Camden County seniors stay nourished and independent.

## Food Pantry

Low-income Camden County seniors **age 60+** can receive a monthly supply of non-perishable food—enough for about six days of meals—to help stretch the grocery budget.

[Food Pantry brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Community_Services.pdf)

### How to request food pantry assistance

Call and ask about the **Food Pantry**. We will confirm eligibility and explain pickup details.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**

## Shut-In Shopping

Homebound Camden County seniors **age 60+** can be matched with a volunteer shopper for bi-weekly grocery and supply runs.

Please note:

- You pay for your purchases
- Service depends on volunteer availability

[Shut-In Shopping brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Community_Services.pdf)

### How to request shut-in shopping

Call and ask for **Shut-In Shopping**. A staff member will explain how the volunteer match works.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**
