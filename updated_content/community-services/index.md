# Community Services

> **Meta description:** SCUCS Community Services offers counseling, food support, and prevention programs to help South Jersey residents stay housed, fed, and financially stable. Find the right program and call to apply.

Life’s challenges—housing stress, food insecurity, unexpected bills—should not leave you without options.

SCUCS Community Services provides practical, respectful support so you can stay independent and connected to your community.

## Counseling programs

Guidance for benefits, housing, shared living arrangements, and Medicare:

- Benefits Counseling
- Housing Counseling
- Housing Navigation
- Shared Housing
- State Health Insurance Program (SHIP)
