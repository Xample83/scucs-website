# Prevention Programs

> **Meta description:** SCUCS Prevention Programs offer heater and A/C help, medical equipment loans, rental assistance, TBRA, and utility aid for Camden and Gloucester County residents. Call to apply for help.

Unexpected costs—a broken heater, back rent, a shut-off notice—can threaten your stability overnight.

SCUCS Prevention Programs provide timely, one-time support so seniors and vulnerable residents can stay safe at home.

## Heating & Cooling

Camden County seniors **age 60+** may receive one-time help with heater or central air repair or replacement when funds are available—so your home stays safe in extreme weather.

[Heating & Cooling brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Emergency_Services.pdf)

### How to apply for heater assistance

Call and ask about **Heating & Cooling assistance**. We will check current funding and guide you through the application.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**

## Lending Closet

Borrow medical equipment if you cannot afford to buy it, including:

- Wheelchairs
- Walkers and canes
- Shower chairs
- Rollators
- Commodes

Limited adult diapers and bed pads may be available depending on donations. Items are loaned temporarily.

[Lending Closet brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Emergency_Services.pdf)

### How to borrow medical equipment

Call and ask about the **Lending Closet**. To donate unused equipment, call the same number—donations are tax-deductible.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**

## Rental Assistance

Camden and Gloucester County residents may receive one-time help with:

- Back rent (to prevent eviction)
- A security deposit (to secure housing)

Help is available when funds allow.

[Rental Assistance brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Emergency_Services.pdf)

### How to apply for rental assistance

Call and ask about **Rental Assistance**.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**

## Tenant-Based Rental Assistance (TBRA)

TBRA offers temporary rental help for low-income households in Camden County (**excluding Camden City**) who are:

- Homeless
- At risk of homelessness
- Fleeing unsafe situations such as domestic violence

You choose an eligible unit, and assistance can move with you. Supportive services—including case management—are available to help you succeed.

[TBRA brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_TBRA.pdf)

### How to apply for TBRA

Call and ask about the **TBRA program**.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**

## Utilities & Fuel

Camden and Gloucester County residents facing hardship may receive one-time help to:

- Stop a utility shut-off
- Get emergency heating fuel when tanks run low

[Utility & Fuel brochure](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Brochure_Emergency_Services.pdf)

### How to apply for utility or fuel help

Call and ask about **Utility Assistance**. Have your bill or disconnect notice ready if possible.

**Call [(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)**
