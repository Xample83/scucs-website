# Developmental Disability Services (DDD)

> **Meta description:** SCUCS DDD services include Support Coordination for adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities across South Jersey. Learn eligibility and how to request a referral.

SCUCS partners with New Jersey’s Division of Developmental Disabilities (DDD) to help adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities live fuller, more independent lives.

## Support Coordination

Our Support Coordination team works with adults across:

- Atlantic County
- Burlington County
- Camden County
- Cumberland County
- Gloucester County
- Mercer County
- Salem County

Together, we help each person build a plan, connect to services, and manage their individual budget.

Supported in partnership with the Camden County Board of Commissioners, Department of Health & Human Services.
