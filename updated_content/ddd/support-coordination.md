# Support Coordination

> **Meta description:** SCUCS Support Coordination helps adults with developmental disabilities in South Jersey build service plans, connect to supports, and manage Medicaid waiver budgets. Request a referral today.

Every person deserves a plan that reflects their goals. Support Coordination pairs adults with intellectual and/or developmental disabilities with a dedicated Support Coordinator who walks beside them.

This is a DDD-funded service. We proudly serve adults in:

- Atlantic County
- Burlington County
- Camden County
- Cumberland County
- Gloucester County
- Mercer County
- Salem County

## What you can expect

- Monthly contact and quarterly visits with an experienced Support Coordinator
- A personalized Individual Service Plan
- Help connecting to services and community supports
- Guidance managing an individualized budget based on your NJCAT assessed level of need

## Who is eligible?

You may qualify if you:

- Live in New Jersey
- Are **21 or older**
- Are enrolled in one of these Medicaid waiver programs:
  - The Supports Program
  - The Community Care Program

## How to request Support Coordination

1. Download and complete our [referral form](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/DDD-Referral-Form.pdf).
2. Call **[(856) 456-1121](tel:8564561121)** to submit your referral or ask questions.

We are ready to walk with you—every step of the way.
