# SEN-HAN Transit Overview

> **Meta description:** SEN-HAN Transit provides transportation for seniors and people with disabilities in Camden and Burlington Counties. Apply for paratransit, view policies, and find county-specific ride information.

SEN-HAN Transit helps older adults and people with disabilities get where they need to go—medical appointments, shopping, and more—across Camden and Burlington Counties.

## How to apply for transportation assistance

1. Download the application for your county (links above).
2. Complete the form.
3. Return it as instructed—or call the reservation number on your county page for help getting started.

## Good to know before you ride

- **Oxygen tanks** — Portable tanks are allowed; you or your escort are responsible for them.
- **Service animals** — Only service animals are permitted. Keep them on the floor at your feet, and mention them when you reserve your ride.
- **Vehicle lifts** — If a lift is not working, we will send another vehicle to complete your trip.
