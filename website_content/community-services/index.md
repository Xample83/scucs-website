# Community Services

> Source: https://scucs.org/community-services/

SCUCS Community Services Department aims to be a steadfast pillar of support, offering tailored interventions and holistic resources to address unique challenges, ultimately empowering individuals to achieve greater self-sufficiency, participate fully in community life, and live with the respect and care deserved.

## Counseling Programs

- Benefits Counseling
- Housing Counseling
- Housing Navigation
- Shared Housing
- State Health Insurance Program (SHIP)

## Food Programs

- Food Pantry
- Shut-In Shopping

## Prevention Programs

- Heating & Cooling
- Lending Closet
- Rental Assistance
- Tenant-Based Rental Assistance
- Utility Assistance
