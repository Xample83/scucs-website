# Shared Housing

> Source: https://scucs.org/services/shared-housing/

SCUCS provides housing assistance for seniors who are 60+ for residents of Camden County who are permanently physically disabled, or for those who can benefit from shared housing.

Our Shared Housing services can provide seniors or those permanently disabled who live in Camden County with information on housing to fit their individual needs. Through strong working relationships with local apartment complexes and subsidized housing projects, SCUCS can assist in locating housing, which decreases housing costs or affordable housing that is lower in cost than regular market priced housing.
