# Burlington County Delaware River Towns Case Management Program

> Source: https://scucs.org/services/case-management/

SCUCS Case Management Program aims to improve the quality of life for seniors and their caregivers. Funding for this program is provided by the Community Foundation of South Jersey.

Case Management Services are provided in the following towns:

- Beverly
- Delanco
- Riverside
- Riverton
- Palmyra

Care Managers can assist with the following:

- Benefits Counseling
- Budget Counseling
- Housing Assistance
- Caregiver Support
- Connection with community resources
- Referrals to community services
- And more based on need
