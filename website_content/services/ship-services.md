## SHIP – State Health Insurance Assistance Program

SHIP service is open to any Burlington County Medicare eligible resident. Our SHIP counselors are trained and certified Medicare experts that provide one-on-one counseling to assist applicants through the Medicare enrollment process, understanding the costs and coverages, and helping to correct billing issues.

To learn more about SHIP counseling services, call 856-456-1121.
