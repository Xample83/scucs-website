# Support Coordination

> Source: https://scucs.org/ddd/support-coordination/

SCUCS is pleased to be able to provide the Support Coordination Program. Through this program, we take pride in the development and implementation of innovative strategies aimed at enhancing the lives of adults with disabilities living in Atlantic, Burlington, Camden, Cumberland, Gloucester, Mercer and Salem Counties.

Support Coordination is a Division of Developmental Disabilities (DDD) funded service providing case management to individuals with intellectual and/or developmental disabilities.

[Printable Referral Form](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/DDD-Referral-Form.pdf)

## Program Highlights

- Monthly contact and quarterly visits with a dependable, experienced Support Coordinator
- Development of an Individual Service Plan
- Linkage to services and supports
- Management of an individualized budget based on NJCAT assessed level of need

## To Be Eligible You Must

- Be a resident of New Jersey
- Be 21 or older
- Be enrolled in one of the following Medicaid Waiver Programs:
  - The Supports Program
  - The Community Care Program
