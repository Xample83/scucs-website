# SEN-HAN Transit – Burlington County

> Source: https://scucs.org/sen-han-transit-burlington-county/

SEN-HAN Transit has been the provider of paratransit services in Burlington County since January 2, 2012. SCUCS has informal agreements with doctors, municipal offices, boarding homes, senior citizen clubs, municipal coordinators on aging, boards of education, child day centers, and health centers to distribute flyers/schedules describing the fixed routes and procedures/information on demand response medical transit.

Having effectively managed and coordinated a consolidated transportation program in Camden County for over thirty years, SCUCS is now providing the same excellent service to residents of Burlington County. SCUCS has experience in developing fixed routes, demand response scheduling, dispatching, developing policies and procedures, locating and acquiring alternative funding, fiscal program accountability and fleet operation.

These rides are available to Burlington County residents 60 years of age or older, or those individuals certified disabled by SCUCS who have completed a [Disabled Application](https://scucs.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/disabledappformBUR0316.pdf) with their physician that has been approved by the administration of SCUCS.

### Non-Emergency Medical Transportation Rides

Appointments can be made for appointments occurring Monday through Friday based on availability. Ride reservations can be made **two days to two weeks** in advance. Reservation lines are open from 9:00 am until 4:00 pm. Call (877) 603-5111 to make a reservation.

### Personal Business Rides

Appointments can be made Monday through Friday based on availability. Personal business ride reservations can be made **three days** in advance and the destination must be within five (5) miles of your home. Reservation lines are open from 9:00 am until 4:00 pm. Call (877) 603-5111 to make a reservation.

## Municipal Shopping Service

SEN-HAN provides weekly shopping service in 38 municipalities in Burlington County to residents 60 years of age and older. These rides are reserved by calling (856) 255-5348 between 6:00 am and 5:00 pm **the working day before your shopping trip**. Leave your name, address, town of residence and the day of the week you will be using the service.

## Veterans Transportation

Transportation for Burlington County veterans is provided Tuesday through Friday each week. The shuttle will arrive at the VA Hospital in Philadelphia by 9:30 am and leave at 2:30 pm to return to the pick-up locations.

| Pick Up Location | Pick-Up Time |
| --- | --- |
| American Legion Post 294, Pemberton | 7:15 am |
| Crossroads Plaza, Lumberton | 7:15 am |
| KMart Shopping Center, Moorestown (Rt. 38) | 8:15 am |
| Shoppes at Cinnaminson, Cinnaminson (Rt. 130) | 8:30 am |

To make a reservation, call (877) 603-5111 by 11:00 am the day before your appointment. You must call 24 hours in advance of your appointment to cancel a pick-up.
